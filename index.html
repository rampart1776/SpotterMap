Welcome to PlanePin

The community map for aviation spotters.

PlanePin is where avgeeks share and discover aircraft sightings from around the world. Every pin is a real X post from a real spotter — browse the map to explore, or add your own spot in seconds.

🗺
Browsing — click any pin on the map to see the original post, aircraft details, and nearby airports.
📍
Spotting — hit + Drop a pin, paste your X post URL, and your sighting lands on the map. AI auto-detects the aircraft type and airport from your post text, or you can manually enter the aircraft type and airport code.
✦
Attribution — credit always stays with the original poster. Every pin links back to the source X post.
